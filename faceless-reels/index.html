10 Faceless AI Reels

Every reel is ready to post: 1080×1920 video with voiceover, music, motion graphics, captions and CTA. Each one comes with 3 hooks, a caption, an auto-DM message and the resource to send. Hook scores are estimates.

Comment-to-DM system

1. Set up the tool (one time, about 20 min)

  1. Switch your Instagram to a Professional account (Creator or Business).
  2. Sign up at manychat.com and connect Instagram. The free plan covers keyword auto-DMs for small accounts.
  3. In Instagram settings, go to Messages and turn on Allow access to messages in connected tools.

2. Build one automation per reel

  1. ManyChat → Automation → New → trigger: "User comments on your post or reel".
  2. Pick the reel. Keyword = the word above (for example COPY). Add lowercase too.
  3. Turn on auto public reply: "Sent! Check your DMs 📩". Public replies boost reach.
  4. DM step 1: "Want the resource? Tap below 👇" with a button. A button tap opens the chat window, so delivery works.
  5. DM step 2: paste the auto-DM message from the card.
  6. Optional step 3: "Follow me to get one new trick daily" + ask for an email for your free AI guide. This builds your list.

3. Get more comments

  • Put the keyword on screen, in the voiceover, and in the caption. All 10 reels do this already.
  • Use one short keyword in capitals. People copy what they see.
  • Pin your own comment: "Comment KEYWORD 👇".
  • Reply to the first 30 to 50 comments by hand in the first hour. Early replies push the reel further.
  • Promise something specific: "the exact prompt", not "more info".
  • Post when your audience is online. Check Insights, then Audience, then Most active times.
  • Share the reel to your Story with a "Comment KEYWORD" sticker.

4. Rules to stay safe

  • Never DM people who didn't comment or message first.
  • Keep DMs helpful. No hard sales pitch in the first message.
  • Test every keyword from a second account before posting.
