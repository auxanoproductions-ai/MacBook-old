# 30 AI Reel Scripts

**Rules for every Reel**
- 15 to 30 seconds. Burned-in captions.
- Hook = on-screen text + spoken line, inside 2 seconds.
- One problem. One fix. One CTA word.
- Auto-DM the prompt or link using ManyChat.
- Hook score = my estimate (curiosity, pain, clarity, mass appeal). It is not a platform metric.

**Viral formats used (1M+ view patterns on Reels)**
- **F1 "Stop doing X":** call out a common mistake.
- **F2 "Secret feature":** "Nobody tells you this."
- **F3 Before/After:** split screen, instant payoff.
- **F4 POV problem:** relatable pain, then the fix.
- **F5 "I shouldn't share this":** gatekeeping tease.
- **F6 Green screen:** you in front of the screen.
